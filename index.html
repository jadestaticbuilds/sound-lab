Sound Lab

Browser music studio

A step sequencer that runs entirely in your browser. No signup, no upload. Tap a square to turn a sound on or off for that step, then press Play, or press Randomize to hear a new pattern right away.

Tracks: Kick, Snare, Hi-hat, Clap, and a Bass that plays a short note pattern. Kit switches the drum character (Classic, 808-style, 909-style) and Bass switches between 808-style and Acid 303-style; both work with any pattern, and Randomize keeps your Kit and Bass choices. Each hit is layered, lightly reverberated, and given a small random velocity change, so repeated hits do not sound like a machine gun. Tap a step once for a normal hit, twice for an accent (gold), three times to clear it. Tap M to mute a track, S to solo it. Song mode plays A, then B, then C, then D, looping. Export records 2 bars of the current pattern as an audio file you can download. Patterns A–D are saved on this device only.
